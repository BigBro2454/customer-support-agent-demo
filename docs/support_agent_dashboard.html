⚖️ Google L5 Architectural Decisions & Trade-Offs
Design Decision Architecture Implemented L5 Trade-off Analysis & Production Rationale
Pre-LLM Guardrail Filtering Deterministic regex & heuristic engine runs before vector DB and LLM inference. Neutralizes prompt injection attacks in 0.1ms with 0 token spend. Prevents adversarial poisoning of embedding cache.
PII Masking at Ingestion Credit cards and SSNs replaced with masked tokens prior to semantic retrieval. Strict compliance with PCI-DSS & GDPR; guarantees unencrypted financial cards never persist in ChromaDB vector indices.
Programmatic Financial Ceiling Hardcoded ceiling ($50.00) overrides LLM recommendations regardless of model confidence. Eliminates hallucinated refunds; ensures corporate financial risk is deterministic rather than probabilistic.
Prioritized HITL Escalation Queue 4-tier priority (Critical, High, Medium, Low) scoring based on legal threats, dollar value, and sentiment. Guarantees human supervisor attention is allocated to urgent churn/legal threats within strict SLA windows.
