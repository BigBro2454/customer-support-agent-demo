# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
- Full refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.
- Partial refunds are available for services used partially, evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
- No refunds after 30 days.

## Process
- For amounts <= $50: The support agent can autonomously process the refund immediately.
- For amounts > $50 and <= $200: The support agent must ask for approval (escalation).
- For amounts > $200: Escalation to human support is mandatory.

## Requirements
- Customer must provide order ID.
- Reason for refund must be stated.
