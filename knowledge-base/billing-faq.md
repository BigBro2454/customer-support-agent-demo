# Billing FAQ

## When will I be charged?
Charges occur automatically on the same day every month or year, depending on your subscription cycle.

## Accepted Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept crypto or cash.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, we will retry after 3 days and 7 days. After 3 failed attempts, the subscription will be paused.
Customers can update their billing information in the account dashboard.

## Can an agent change my payment method?
No. Support agents cannot manually update payment details due to security reasons. The agent should direct the customer to the account dashboard.
